The [OpenJS Foundation CNA](https://cna.openjsf.org/) is taking a [coordinated end-of-year break from December 18, 2026 to January 4, 2027](https://github.com/openjs-foundation/security-wg/issues/355). The Express security team is joining the pause for the same window, as we did for the [previous coordinated break](/blog/2026-09-09-joining-the-openjs-cna-security-break). That one passed without security incidents or escalations, and the team came back at full capacity, so we are glad to do it again.

## What this means for Express

From December 18, 2026 to January 4, 2027:

- Triage, patch development, advisory validation, CVE assignment, and security releases are paused.
- Channels stay open, so you can still file reports, but please do not expect a response until we return on **January 5**.
- Where possible, please hold non urgent reports until after the break, and keep following coordinated disclosure.

**Emergency exception:** if a vulnerability is being actively exploited, or poses immediate and serious risk, we will still respond. Reach out in the **#express** channel on the [OpenJS Foundation Slack](https://slack-invite.openjsf.org/), and mark your message as urgent. Please note that this is a public channel: keep the message high level and do not share confidential information or vulnerability details there. We will move the conversation to a private channel.

We will be back on **January 5, 2027**. Thank you for following coordinated disclosure.

To the maintainers who build on Express, and to the wider community: we hope you find your own moments to rest and recharge over the holidays too.

The Express Security Team
